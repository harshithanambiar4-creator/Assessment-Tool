## Rules from the template's "How to use" slides
- **General:** keep it simple, clean and uncluttered; leave empty space around text and graphics; avoid flashy transitions and text fly-ins.
- **Text:** (course decision: **headings in Title Case**; body text and labels in sentence case)
  - Use Aptos.
  - Limit the words on each screen and keep only the essentials.
  - **Use sentence case, not ALL CAPITALS.**
- **Colour:**
  - Use the primary colours first: navy, teal, green.
  - Limit the number of colours on one screen, and keep strong contrast.
  - **On light slides, titles and headlines are Pro Navy or Pro Teal. On dark (navy) slides, titles and headlines are Pro Green.**
- **Layout:**
  - **Logo top right.**
  - Start from a predefined layout before building a custom one.
  - Use the pre-designed formats for headshots, tables, charts and timelines.
- **Graphics:** use few, high-quality graphics per slide, and no noisy animation.

## Master elements (every slide)
- Resource Pro logo top right. Footer bottom left: "Proprietary • Confidential". Slide number bottom right.
- A thin green-to-teal gradient line along the bottom edge.
- Slide size 16:9 (13.33 × 7.5 in), the same ratio as Storyline's 1280 × 720.

## Logic-structure library (Audited 2024 deck)
1. **Parallel (slides 3–23):** 4 to 7 equal items in icon circles, numbered tiles, hexagons, cards or arrows.
2. **Comparison (24–27):** "vs." rows, paired bars with icons, a balance scale.
3. **Sequential (28–39):** numbered steps along a wave, chevrons, arrow cards or a timeline (4 to 6 steps).
4. **Deductive / total-to-parts (40–53):** a centre hub with satellites, a pyramid or podium, a segmented ring.
5. **Loop (54–61):** cycles of 4 to 6 steps (arrows in a circle, segmented donut).
6. **Complex (62–65):** combined diagrams.

Style across the library:
- Elements are coloured in order: teal → green → blue → grey (→ coral for a fifth).
- Numbers sit in rounded tiles or circles.
- Icons are thin line icons inside circles.
- Each item has a short bold label above one or two lines of body text.
